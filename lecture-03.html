What the chapter covers

The 60–80% of the work: grain and keys, joins that do not explode, the five cleaning steps, the three missingness mechanisms, panels, transformations and pipelines you can re-run.

Outline

§1Quality and preparation at a glance
Why preparation dominates, the five dimensions of quality, grain — what does one row mean — layering and data contracts, the five cleaning steps, five rules for reproducibility
§2Combine and reshape
Append and merge, join types and union, cardinality and why row counts explode, key normalisation, safe merges with validation, wide and long layouts, pivot tables, the reshaping toolbox
§3Data cleaning
Duplicates and irrelevant records, structural errors, outliers and three detection rules, the five steps in code, messy strings, near duplicates and record linkage, the audit trail
§4Missing data
Where missingness comes from, MCAR / MAR / MNAR with simulated examples, three strategies, the imputation family, scoring imputations, what imputation costs, missingness as signal, the decision tree
§5Panels and balance
Balanced against unbalanced panels, nominal balance and real gaps, why panels go unbalanced, diagnosing in Stata and pandas, interpolation, a prefecture-level population case
§6Transformation, tools and engineering
Variable transformations and taming skew, unit uniformity, pandas against Stata commands, Polars and DuckDB for bigger tables, pandera and Great Expectations, dbt and feature stores, LLM-assisted cleaning, de-identification, drift monitoring
§7Projects and wrap-up
Project 1 requirements, the one-page proposal, the quality report template, the 30-item preparation checklist, toolbox, glossary, a reproducibility drill

Learning objectives

  • State the grain of a table and the key that identifies a row, before writing a single join;
  • Choose a join type from the question, and detect the cardinality explosion that follows a careless one;
  • Run the five cleaning steps in order, and keep an audit trail that another analyst can replay;
  • Distinguish MCAR, MAR and MNAR, and justify deletion, imputation or modelling the missingness;
  • Balance and interpolate a panel without inventing observations that never existed;
  • Package preparation as a pipeline: typed, tested, versioned, and re-runnable from raw data to analysis table.
