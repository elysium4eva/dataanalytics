What the chapter covers

Why analytics comes before modelling: the DIKW ladder, the 5 V, the four levels of analytics, the 2026 AI landscape, and the failure modes that sink data projects.

Learning objectives

  • Explain the DIKW ladder, the 5 V of big data and the four levels of analytics, and place a marketing question on that ladder;
  • Map the 2026 AI landscape — foundation models, reasoning models, agents, retrieval — and say what each one can and cannot do;
  • Distinguish supervised, unsupervised, self-supervised and reinforcement learning, and state the data each needs;
  • Read a reported metric critically: what was the split, what was the baseline, what would make the number optimistic;
  • Describe how an LLM generates text, where hallucination comes from, and what retrieval or tool use fixes;
  • Assess data quality, bias and compliance before analysis, and declare AI use honestly in your own work.
